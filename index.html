Dental clinic / Lead follow up

BrightSmile Dental Clinic

Situation: People ask about treatments on the website, but the clinic replies hours later and many never book.

What the system does: It replies within 1 minute with a booking link, reminds patients before their visit, invites no shows to rebook, and asks for a review afterwards.

Result: No inquiry is left unanswered, and the owner gets an alert for every new lead and booking.

Service business / Client onboarding

SwiftDesk Virtual Assistance

Situation: A new client signs up, then details get lost in scattered emails and the first week starts late.

What the system does: A landing page collects the inquiry. Then the system books the discovery call, follows up the proposal, sends a welcome email with one onboarding form, and checks in with the client every week.

Result: Every client gets the same organized start, and an unhappy client is noticed early.

Email / Important messages

AI Email Summary to WhatsApp

Situation: Important emails and action items are mixed with routine messages.

What the system does: It reads each new email, picks out what matters, and sends a short summary to WhatsApp.

Example message: Important: Project review meeting. Action: confirm attendance. Deadline: Monday, 5:00 PM.

Scheduling / Meeting requests

WhatsApp Replies and Calendar Confirmation

Situation: Someone asks for a meeting by email, and you need to check your calendar before answering.

What the system does: It checks your calendar, tells you on WhatsApp if the time is free, and prepares the confirmation when you say yes.

Example message: Available: Wednesday, 10:00 to 11:00 AM (GMT+3). Reply to confirm the meeting.
